# OneOfZero Positioning

## Who we are

OneOfZero Systems is a New Zealand-based development consultancy. Founder: Paul Alejo. Solo operator with deep AWS, TypeScript / Next.js, and clinic-operations focus. We build production operations portals for healthcare-adjacent clinics, starting with Midland Sleep.

## What we sell

```text
Production Operations Portals
  — for clinics that have outgrown spreadsheets and dependence on
    third-party ordering systems but cannot justify enterprise SaaS.

  — bounded scope, fixed price, monthly retainer.

  — operational outcome, not a website.

  — productized as a clinic-by-clinic offer once Midland is the case study.
```

## What we DO NOT sell

```text
- websites
- marketing builds
- raw "consulting hours"
- AI chatbots / agents (the "agentic OS" is the operating layer, not bots)
- generic SaaS subscriptions
- enterprise sales motions
- offshore-ish development services
```

## Who we sell to

```text
Primary now           NZ sleep clinics like Midland (CPAP entitlement workflows)
Adjacent              NZ allied healthcare clinics with funded supply patterns
                       (orthotics, mobility aids, hearing aids)
Adjacent              private clinics needing patient + admin portals
Year 2+               multi-clinic groups in the same vertical
```

## Why we win

```text
1. Clinic operations specificity — most NZ devs build websites, not portals
2. Privacy + HIPC discipline — most builders don't think about audit by design
3. Productized SOP layer — Midland OS v1 reduces the ongoing risk to the clinic
4. Owner-operator focus — the clinic talks to the person doing the work
5. Reusable infra — multi-clinic is a configuration shift, not a new build
6. Retainer-aligned incentives — we win when the clinic operates well
```

## Three-line elevator

> OneOfZero builds bounded-scope production operations portals for NZ healthcare-adjacent clinics. We replace third-party ordering dependencies with a portal the clinic can actually operate, plus a documented operating layer (Midland OS v1) so the clinic isn't dependent on us. Anchor: NZD 42k build, NZD 2,300/month retainer.

## Brand guard rails

```text
- calm, clinical, simple — never salesy
- "production operations portal" — never "website"
- "operating layer" — never "agentic OS chatbot"
- "Midland Sleep is our case study" — never name a patient
- "we are a development consultancy" — never "agency"
- always lead with operational outcome, never feature list
```

## What we are NOT

```text
- a marketing agency
- an AI product company
- a SaaS startup
- a 24/7 SLA support shop
- a clinical SaaS provider
- a legal / compliance certification body
```
