# Mission

## What we do

OneOfZero Systems builds healthcare-adjacent operations portals for NZ clinics. Custom software where the patient experience is calm and clinical, the admin experience reduces hours of phone-and-spreadsheet work, and the data handling meets HIPC 2020 from day one.

## Who we serve

Specialist NZ clinics that:
- run patient supply / reorder workflows on top of government funding
- depend on a third-party portal (ALTER, Healthlink, etc.) they don't control
- have a non-technical admin team carrying the operational load
- want to grow but cannot scale on phone calls and spreadsheets

Sleep, allied health, mobility/disability supply, optical reorder, and similar funded-supply categories.

## Why us

```text
- We build production operations portals, not marketing websites.
- We start with one wedge — controlled import + admin review + export + backup —
  not a 12-feature platform.
- We codify HIPC 2020 from day one.
- We operate Midland OS beside the portal: SOPs, decisions, risks, learnings,
  handover. The clinic gets an operating layer, not just code.
- We commit to one clinic, completely, before pursuing the next.
```

## What we will not do

```text
- generic web design / marketing builds
- consumer SaaS products
- AI chatbots replacing clinical judgement
- anything that processes real patient data through unreviewed AI tools
- "just a website" projects priced as freelance gigs
- unlimited support retainers that absorb all our time
- unpaid scope expansion
```

## Strategic target

Year 1: Midland Sleep delivered, retainer running, case study packaged.
Year 2: Two more NZ sleep clinics, multi-clinic Waikato conversation.
Year 3: Productized clinic operations portal offer, reusable Terraform + onboarding pack, 4–6 clinic operating layer.

## Tone

Brutal where useful. Calm where required. Never salesy in patient-facing surfaces, ever.
