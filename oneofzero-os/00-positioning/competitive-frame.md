# Competitive Frame

## What we are competing against

Not other agencies. Mostly:

```text
1. Spreadsheets + phone + email          (the status quo)
2. Third-party portals (ALTER, etc.)     (rented platforms with no control)
3. Generic CRM bolted onto clinical work (wrong shape)
4. Internal-IT custom builds              (over-promised, under-delivered)
5. Big-vendor health platforms            (priced for hospitals, not clinics)
```

## Where each competitor breaks down

### vs spreadsheets + phone

```text
Their strength: zero capital cost, everyone knows it
Our edge:       30+ hours/week of admin time wasted, no audit trail, no funder
                evidence, NHI-in-spreadsheet privacy risk, no patient self-service
```

### vs third-party portals (ALTER, similar)

```text
Their strength: turnkey, "someone else's problem"
Our edge:       no control over UX, expensive, slow to change, vendor lock-in,
                doesn't surface clinic-specific entitlement logic, no clinic
                ownership of audit data
```

### vs generic CRM (Salesforce Health, etc.)

```text
Their strength: features galore
Our edge:       wrong shape — generic pipeline thinking, expensive licences,
                cannot encode the entitlement layer cleanly, requires admin
                staff to become CRM operators
```

### vs internal IT custom build

```text
Their strength: in-house ownership
Our edge:       solo or sub-3-person teams routinely under-deliver on
                healthcare-grade compliance; HIPC 2020 is harder than it looks;
                no operating layer beside the code; no SOPs; no handover discipline
```

### vs big-vendor health platforms

```text
Their strength: enterprise-grade, full-stack
Our edge:       priced for hospitals (NZD 200k+/yr), 12-month implementations,
                rigid workflows, contracts that lock in 3+ years
```

## Our positioning sentence

> A production operations portal + Midland OS — purpose-built for NZ specialist clinics with funded-supply workflows. Less than a hospital platform, more than a website. Compliant from day one, operationally usable, and packaged for handover.

## What we never claim

```text
- "AI-powered" (it isn't, and shouldn't be near patient data)
- "Replaces your clinical software" (it doesn't)
- "Increases revenue" without showing the funding-utilisation case
- "Fully automated" (it is controlled, not automated)
```

## What we lead with

```text
- specific Midland numbers — 35 hours/week saved, NZD 80k/yr return, 6,000 patients
- evidence pack — screenshots, audit log examples, redacted exports
- Privacy Officer sign-off and HIPC compliance posture
- one-page architecture diagram + scope discipline visible upfront
- monthly retainer with clear boundaries
```
