# OneOfZero — Elevator Pitch Assets

## 30-Second Pitch (for networking / intro calls)

> "OneOfZero builds production operations portals for NZ healthcare clinics.
> We replace the manual spreadsheet-and-phone workflow that most clinics run
> on with a compliant, audit-safe patient portal — so patients can self-serve
> and admin staff can see everything clearly without calling ALTER or digging
> through Excel files. We're currently live with a CPAP clinic in Waikato
> and expanding to other NZ clinics from Year 2."

---

## Problem Statement (for proposals)

> Most NZ clinics managing government-funded patient equipment are running on
> a third-party ordering portal, a spreadsheet, and a phone line. There is no
> patient-facing visibility, no audit trail, and no compliance layer.
> When a Privacy Officer asks "how do you track NHI access?" the answer is
> usually "we don't." That is the problem OneOfZero solves.

---

## The Offer (for Midland-style clinics)

> A production patient portal that:
> - Replaces ALTER / manual ordering
> - Shows entitlement YES/NO (government-compliant)
> - Imports patient records from your existing spreadsheet data
> - Gives admin staff a clean review and export layer
> - Is fully HIPC 2020-aligned with a Privacy Officer sign-off pack included
> - Goes live in 8 weeks

---

## Objection Handling

**"We already have ALTER / a system."**
> "ALTER is a third-party dependency you don't control. This gives you your
> own system, your own data, your own audit trail — under your domain."

**"Can't we just use a form or a spreadsheet?"**
> "A form doesn't give patients visibility into their entitlement status.
> A spreadsheet doesn't give you an audit trail for the Privacy Commissioner."

**"It sounds expensive."**
> "A Privacy Act breach or an HIPC complaint costs more. And the retainer
> pays for itself if it reduces even 10 staff calls per week."

**"Can you build it faster / cheaper?"**
> "I can reduce scope — but I won't reduce compliance. The HIPC requirements
> are non-negotiable for a healthcare-adjacent system. What I can do is
> phase the build so you get the core working fast and add features later."
