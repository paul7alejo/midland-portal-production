## How we sell Phase 1

```text
"Midland Sleep Phase 1 Production Operations Portal"

Outcome promise (in Midland's words, not ours):
  - replace your dependency on ALTER for ordering
  - give your admin staff a portal that handles the entitlement question
  - get audit data so you can pursue NZD 2M government funding
  - get a backup-able, exportable, owned data layer
  - meet HIPC 2020 from day one
```

## How we frame retainer

```text
NZD 2,300 / month incl GST — 3 months notice to cancel.

Includes:
  - maintenance + minor fixes
  - AWS / hosting monitoring
  - Cognito user admin assistance
  - 1-hour monthly improvement review
  - support questions (reasonable volume)
  - audit log spot-checks
  - backup verification

Excludes:
  - new features (separate scope)
  - shop / Stripe / inventory / mobile (Phase 2+)
  - patient invitation automation
  - bulk patient email
  - clinical or legal responsibility
```

## How we say no to scope creep

```text
"That's a great Phase 2 conversation — let me add it to the feature backlog
 with the right priority. For now, I'm protecting June 30 by keeping the
 build to the agreed scope."

"To do that well I'd want to scope it as a separate quote — let me send
 a one-pager next week. The retainer covers maintenance, not new builds."
```

## Discounting policy

```text
- No discounts on the anchor (NZD 42,000) without explicit reasoning in
  decision-log.md.
- No "first project" pricing — Midland already has the most aggressive
  pricing we should ever offer; future clinics pay more.
- Multi-clinic deals can include implementation discount per clinic, but
  retainer per clinic stays at anchor.
- Speed-to-pay incentive ok: 5% off Phase 1 build if 50% paid at deposit.
```

## Payment terms

```text
- 15% deposit before kickoff, NZD 6,300
- net 14 on milestone invoices
- invoicing in NZD incl GST
- preferred: bank transfer
- late-payment: 2% per month after 30 days, written into contract
```

## Scope expansion pricing rules

```text
- Anything not in the agreed milestone list is a change request.
- Change requests get a one-pager: scope, price, timeline, dependencies.
- Change requests do NOT extend the June 30 date unless I explicitly agree.
- A change request that risks the date is declined politely.
```

## Insurance + protection

```text
[ ] Professional indemnity insurance — annual, before production
    healthcare-adjacent support
[ ] Cyber insurance — annual, before production
[ ] Contract reviewed by an NZ commercial lawyer — one-off NZD 1,000–3,500
[ ] Limitation of liability cap in contract (typically 2× annual fees)
[ ] No clinical responsibility — explicit in contract
[ ] Privacy Officer responsibility stays with Midland — explicit in contract
```
