---

## Before (ALTER + Spreadsheet Workflow)

| Pain | Detail |
|---|---|
| Patient ordering | Patients call or email Midland. Staff manually process via ALTER. |
| Data management | Biomedical-style spreadsheets, manually managed |
| Entitlement visibility | Staff check ALTER manually per patient |
| Audit trail | None or manual spreadsheet notes |
| Staff time | High — manual data entry, ALTER navigation, phone calls |
| Compliance posture | No formal HIPC audit trail on patient data access |

---

## After (Midland OS Portal — Phase 1B)

| Outcome | Detail |
|---|---|
| Patient ordering | Self-service reorder requests via portal. Patients log in on any device. |
| Data management | Controlled import from Biomedical spreadsheet → DynamoDB audit trail |
| Entitlement visibility | YES/NO entitlement status visible to patients and staff. Govt-compliant display. |
| Audit trail | Append-only DynamoDB audit log. Every NHI access, every import, every export. |
| Staff time | Reduced — admin reviews import batches, not individual patient calls |
| Compliance posture | HIPC 2020-aligned. Privacy Officer sign-off obtained before go-live. |
