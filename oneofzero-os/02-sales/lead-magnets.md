# Lead Magnets

> Hormozi-style: give the secrets, sell the implementation.
> Use Midland delivery as the substrate for assets that attract the next clinic.

## Lead magnet ideas (priority order)

### 1. Clinic Spreadsheet-to-Portal Readiness Checklist

```text
Format:    1-page PDF
Audience:  clinic ops leaders running on phone+spreadsheet+third-party portal
Promise:   "30 questions to know if your clinic is ready to leave ALTER (or similar)"

Sections:
  - data ownership questions (who owns the NHI, the export, the audit trail)
  - workflow questions (how many phone calls per reorder, who validates eligibility)
  - compliance questions (HIPC 2020 posture, Privacy Officer in place)
  - readiness questions (sample export, clinical sign-off, training capacity)

CTA:       "Want a 1-hour walk-through of where your clinic sits? Book a call."
```

### 2. CPAP Entitlement Workflow Audit

```text
Format:    paid 2-hour deep-dive (NZD 750 incl GST) — or free if it converts to Phase 0
Audience:  sleep clinic clinical leads
Promise:   "Where your funded supply pathway leaks time, money, and audit data"

Deliverable:
  - 10-page report
  - workflow diagram (current vs proposed)
  - one-page prioritised fix list
  - quote for Phase 0 if there's a fit

CTA:       Discovery + Phase 1 quote
```

### 3. Sleep Clinic Admin Bottleneck Review

```text
Format:    free 30-min call + follow-up email summary
Audience:  clinic admins, ops leads
Promise:   "Three things eating your week, and what you can do about each"

Outcome:
  - empathy + listen
  - practical low-cost suggestions (might not be us)
  - if there's a fit, propose a paid Phase 0
```

### 4. Patient Reorder Experience Teardown

```text
Format:    free 1-page write-up
Audience:  clinic operators curious about patient self-service
Promise:   "What works in the Midland portal — and what's deliberately NOT there"

Includes:
  - screenshots (with permission)
  - the 4 things older patients find easy
  - the 3 dark patterns we explicitly refused (countdowns, urgency, upsells)
```

### 5. HIPC 2020 Posture Document Template

```text
Format:    Word template + 3-page how-to
Audience:  clinic Privacy Officers
Promise:   "Your HIPC 2020 posture across 13 rules, in a format the funder accepts"

This is genuinely useful. We give it away. The implementation help is paid.
```

## How to deploy them

```text
LinkedIn          weekly post + CTA to opt-in for the lead magnet
Email             nurture sequence after opt-in (3 emails over 2 weeks)
Speaking          NZ healthcare ops events — free magnet + opt-in form
Partnerships      NZ Privacy Officer networks, ACC adjacent
Referral          Midland-direct referral after go-live
```

## Production cadence

```text
Quarterly         refresh top 2 magnets with newest Midland numbers
Monthly           one new short-form post (LinkedIn) referencing a magnet
Yearly            decide which magnets to retire and which to invest in
```

## Hormozi-style framing notes

```text
- Lead with the specific number, not the abstract benefit
- Promise outcome, not feature
- Be specific about audience (NZ specialist clinic ops lead, not "clinics")
- Show the work (case study evidence) before asking for the call
- Don't bait-and-switch — the magnet content is genuinely useful on its own
- The implementation help is what's paid; the knowledge is given
```
