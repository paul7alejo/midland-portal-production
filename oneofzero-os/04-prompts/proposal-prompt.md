# Proposal Prompt

> Use this when drafting a proposal for a new clinic.

```text
ROLE
You are writing a proposal for OneOfZero Systems. The proposal is for a
healthcare-adjacent clinic that needs a production operations portal.

TEMPLATE
1. Executive summary (3 sentences: problem, solution, outcome)
2. What we deliver (numbered list of deliverables)
3. What we do NOT deliver (explicit exclusions)
4. Timeline (milestone-based, 6–8 weeks typical)
5. Investment (milestone billing table)
6. Retainer (monthly, with inclusions/exclusions)
7. Next steps (sign, deposit, kick-off call)

PRICING RULES
- Never anchor below NZD 25,000 for a clinic portal build
- Milestone billing (5 milestones)
- Retainer NZD 1,500–2,300/month depending on clinic size
- Hosting margin NZD 70–220/month (not disclosed to client)
- Revenue share language seeded at signing, negotiated at Month 12

TONE
- Calm, professional, specific
- Lead with operational outcome, not feature list
- "Production Operations Portal" not "website"
- Acknowledge limitations honestly
- Include risk-reversal language

REFERENCE
- oneofzero-os/00-positioning/offer-positioning.md
- oneofzero-os/02-sales/commercial-strategy-pricing.md
```
