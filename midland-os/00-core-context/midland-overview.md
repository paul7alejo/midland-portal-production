# Midland Sleep Portal Overview

The Midland Sleep portal is a production operations portal, not a basic public website. Its purpose is to support clinic workflows, patient equipment operations, controlled patient-data imports, staff review, and future patient-facing services.

Phase 1 is focused on the foundation needed before broader operational use:

- Admin and patient portal foundations
- Controlled biomedical spreadsheet imports
- Admin visibility for imported patient records
- Safe handling patterns for NHI and patient information
- Standard operating procedures for release, import, and review workflows

OneOfZero's role is technical implementation and operations platform partnership. That includes building the application, shaping technical workflows, supporting deployment readiness, and documenting operational procedures.

Clinical, legal, privacy, funding, and patient-care decisions remain Midland Sleep's responsibility. Documentation in this repository supports operational clarity, but it is not a legal, clinical, or compliance sign-off.
