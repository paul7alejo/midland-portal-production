# Midland OS Phase Map

## Phase 1 Foundation / MVP

Establish the core admin and patient portal foundation, safe import workflows, admin visibility, and production operating procedures.

Current status:

- Day 28 controlled real import execution complete
- Day 29 admin imported patient visibility complete
- Day 30 production rehearsal/docs in progress

## Phase 2 Admin Operations

Expand staff workflows for patient review, entitlement visibility, notes, operational task handling, and safer administration of imported records.

## Phase 3 CPAP Store / Entitlement Checkout

Build patient and staff ordering workflows around funded entitlements, patient-paid items, eligibility rules, and checkout controls.

## Phase 4 Inventory and Fulfilment

Connect ordering workflows to stock, dispatch, fulfilment status, replenishment visibility, and operational reporting.

## Phase 5 Growth / Referrals / Retention

Support referral intake, patient retention workflows, recalls, outreach, service reminders, and growth reporting.

## Phase 6 Multi-Clinic SaaS

Generalise the platform for multi-clinic usage, tenant isolation, configurable workflows, support models, and repeatable onboarding.
