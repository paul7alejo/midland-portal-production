**What was demonstrated**

We walked through the complete Phase 2D patient supply request lifecycle:

- A patient logged into the portal, selected CPAP supply items, confirmed a delivery address,
  and submitted a request.
- The request appeared immediately in the Admin Orders work queue under the Active tab.
- Staff updated the request status through the review cycle — Reviewing, Approved, Sent — and
  the patient saw clear, plain-language copy at each stage on their portal.
- Staff marked the request as Delivered. The request moved automatically to the Completed tab,
  and the patient's portal unlocked the request form so they could submit a new request when
  they next need supplies.
- We opened the patient drawer from the Orders queue, showing that staff can view a patient's
  device, mask, and record alongside the active request without switching screens.
- We reviewed the Audit Log, which records every status change with the staff member's email,
  the patient MSID, the previous status, and the new status — a complete trail for every action.

---

**What was not demonstrated as live**

To be clear about the current scope:

- Automatic patient email or SMS notifications are not live. The patient currently refreshes
  their portal to see status updates. Automated notifications are Phase 2E (see below).
- No inventory is reserved or deducted when a request is submitted or dispatched.
- No entitlement balance is adjusted in ACC or any other system. Estimate fields in the admin
  view are for staff reference only.
- No payment is taken through the portal.
- No automated fulfilment or dispatch task is created.
- Clinical eligibility is not validated by the system — that remains a staff responsibility
  during request review.

**Phase 2E — delayed patient notifications**

The next proposed step is Phase 2E: a delayed email notification system that sends the patient
a brief, plain-language update 30 minutes after a staff member changes a request status.
The delay gives staff a correction window before the patient is contacted.

The specification is complete and ready to build. We are waiting for your answers to eight
decisions before starting. I have attached [or: included below] the decision capture sheet.

**The one item that may need a brief check before the meeting is Decision 7:** whether sending
status update emails to patients is treated as transactional communication under the NZ
Unsolicited Electronic Messages Act 2007, and does not require separate opt-in consent.
We believe transactional status updates are clearly exempt, but this is your organisation's
legal position to confirm — not ours. If you have an adviser who handles this, a short query
to them is the safest path.

All other decisions can be made by you and your team without external input.
